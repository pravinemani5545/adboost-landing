## The three deficits that keep you at position 50+

1. **Authority routing (architecture)** — the two most valuable section pairs never link to each other (advertising↔industries), all 29 glossary terms share one hardcoded, often-wrong "related" block, blog posts never link to money pages, and the homepage body links to almost nothing. Link equity cannot flow to the pages that need it.
2. **Content that doesn't survive evaluation (pSEO)** — 31 glossary URLs at ~110 unique words in 65-70% boilerplate; 12 advertising pages sharing a verbatim skeleton with an incomplete matrix. The industries pages (7% sibling overlap, dense proprietary data) prove the site CAN do it — they're the template.
3. **Zero off-site authority** — no backlinks, no YouTube/Reddit/Wikipedia footprint (the strongest AI-citation predictors), no Bing presence until today. This is why a technically-clean site gets neither rankings nor AEO leads.
