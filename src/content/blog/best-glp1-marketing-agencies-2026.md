Choosing a marketing agency for a GLP-1 or weight-loss brand is a higher-stakes decision than for almost any other DTC category. The wrong pick doesn't just waste budget — it gets your ad account disabled in the most scrutinized advertising vertical on the internet. This guide covers the criteria that actually matter and the agencies worth knowing in 2026.

*This is our honest read of the landscape. We're one of the agencies listed, and we've tried to be fair about where each type of partner fits.*

## What to look for in a GLP-1 marketing agency

Before any list, the criteria. A good GLP-1 agency should have:

1. **Health-vertical compliance expertise, not general DTC knowledge.** They should know Meta's Health & Wellness policy, LegitScript certification, and the compounded-GLP-1 rules by heart. Ask them what changed after the 35-state-AG action — if they don't know, keep looking.
2. **Compliant creative production at volume.** GLP-1 accounts need 30–60 fresh creatives a month to hold CAC, and every one must clear review. Ask to see their compliant-creative process.
3. **Clean, blended attribution.** They should measure fully-loaded paying-patient CAC (not consult CAC) on blended MER, not platform-reported ROAS.
4. **A retention lens.** In GLP-1, 50–65% of non-diabetic patients discontinue within 12 months. An agency that only optimizes first-fill CAC is optimizing the wrong number.
5. **Proof in *your* vertical.** GLP-1 economics differ from supplements or med spa. Look for GLP-1-specific results.

## How to run the actual evaluation

Once you have a shortlist, put each agency through the same test:

- Ask them to walk through a **compliant GLP-1 creative** they've shipped and why it cleared review.
- Ask how they'd handle **the async intake and prescribing flow** off-platform.
- Ask for **fully-loaded paying-patient CAC** on a real account, and how they measure it.
- Ask what their plan is **if Meta flags the account** — the answer reveals how much they actually operate in this vertical.

## The bottom line

The best GLP-1 marketing agency for your brand is the one that treats compliance as the strategy, ships compliant creative at volume, and measures true blended CAC with a retention lens. For most early-to-growth GLP-1 brands, that means a health-specialist performance partner over a generalist or a broad full-service shop.
