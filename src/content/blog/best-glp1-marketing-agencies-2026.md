GLP-1 marketing agencies fall into three groups: health-specialist performance agencies, full-service healthcare agencies, and clinic-focused marketers. Each agency below shows GLP-1 or medical weight loss work on its own website. Choose by your model (a DTC telehealth program or an in-person clinic), the channels you need, and how each describes its compliance process.

Choosing a marketing agency for a [GLP-1 or weight-loss brand](/industries/glp-1-weight-loss/) is a higher-stakes decision than for almost any other DTC category. The wrong pick doesn't just waste budget; it can get your ad account disabled in one of the most scrutinized advertising categories there is.

## Which type of agency fits a DTC GLP-1 program, and which fits a clinic?

The agencies above split by who they say they serve, and that matters more than any ranking:

- **DTC telehealth GLP-1 programs** (online intake, prescribing partners, refills shipped): you need paid social and search that handle drug-name rules, lifecycle email and SMS for continuation, and an intake funnel that converts. On their own pages, Healthcare Success names virtual-first platforms, PELORA names telehealth programs, Sweat Pants Agency names telehealth brands, and Avenue Z names telehealth, weight loss and GLP-1 brands.
- **Clinics and med spas offering GLP-1 in person** (people searching for a medical weight loss marketing agency usually sit here): you need local search, Google Business Profile, location keywords, reviews and booking. Sagapixel names medical spas and weight loss doctors, Target Patients MD names clinics and bariatric practices, PELORA names licensed clinics, and Accelerated Digital Media's case study is a multi-location clinic group.

If you run both a clinic network and an online program, ask each shortlisted agency which of the two it has more published work in.

## What should you check that is specific to GLP-1?

General agency diligence (portfolio, reporting, the first 30 days) applies to every health brand; we keep those questions in our [comparison of generalist DTC agencies and health specialists](/compare/generalist-dtc-agency-alternative/). For GLP-1, add these:

1. **Ask how they handle drug names.** On Meta, naming semaglutide or tirzepatide needs LegitScript certification plus Meta authorization, with US, Canada or New Zealand targeting and 18+ ([GLP-1 on Meta](/advertising/glp-1-on-meta/)). On Google, drug names may appear in ad text in those three countries, but keyword-targeting them needs certification ([GLP-1 on Google](/advertising/glp-1-on-google/)). A good answer separates the two platforms.
2. **Ask what changed for them in July 2026.** Meta deleted its written ban on side-by-side weight-loss comparisons, but prescription weight-loss ads follow its Drugs and Pharmaceuticals standard, which says nothing about imagery ([what Meta changed](/advertising/#meta-july-2026)). An agency that runs transformation creative on an Rx program should be able to say why.
3. **Ask how they talk about compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide.** That is FDA and FTC territory, not an ad-platform rule; see the [FDA's guidance for telehealth companies promoting compounded drugs](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-telehealth-companies-what-know-when-promoting-compounded-drugs).
4. **Ask what they measure after the first fill.** A GLP-1 program's economics depend on continuation and refills, so an agency that reports only first-fill CAC is reporting half the picture. Our [GLP-1 CAC benchmarks](/blog/telehealth-cac-benchmarks-2026/) show why.
5. **Ask for GLP-1 work in your model.** A clinic case study tells you little about a national DTC program, and the reverse.

### How do GLP-1 marketing agencies price their work?

Of the seven agencies above, PELORA is the only one that publishes prices on the pages we read: flat monthly retainers starting at $3,500, a one-time setup fee, and ad spend paid to Google and Meta directly, not marked up ([PELORA's GLP-1 page](https://peloramarketing.com/medical-weight-loss-marketing)). The others don't state prices there, so ask each for its fee model in writing, including what happens to fees as ad spend grows.

### Is a medical weight loss marketing agency the same as a GLP-1 marketing agency?

The terms overlap. Agencies that say "medical weight loss" on their sites tend to describe in-person clinics, local search and booking; agencies that say "GLP-1 brands" or "telehealth" tend to describe online programs. Read who each agency says it serves before you shortlist it.
