Telehealth patient acquisition is deceptively hard. The economics look like DTC ecommerce, but the compliance layer, the certification requirements, and the async-to-sync patient handoff are entirely different — and the wrong agency will burn budget on all three. Here's how to choose a telehealth marketing agency in 2026 and the agencies worth knowing.

*This is our honest read of the landscape. We're one of the agencies listed, and we've tried to be fair about where each type fits.*

## What to look for in a telehealth marketing agency

The criteria that separate a real telehealth partner from a generalist:

1. **LegitScript and platform-certification fluency.** They should know that Google requires LegitScript plus its own Prescription drug services certification, and that Meta needs LegitScript plus authorization. If they've never sequenced a certification, they'll cost you a launch window. (See our [LegitScript certification guide](/blog/legitscript-certification-telehealth-guide/).)
2. **Condition-specific funnels, not a generic homepage.** Dedicated condition landing pages convert around 8%+ versus ~2–3% for a generic page. The agency should build a symptom quiz → condition page → membership, not send traffic to your homepage.
3. **True paying-patient CAC.** Most telehealth brands quote *consult* CAC, which runs 40–60% below the real paying-patient number. A good agency separates the two and measures blended MER.
4. **A retention and cross-sell plan.** Telehealth economics only work because episodic care becomes subscription revenue — refill cadence, condition stacking, multi-condition cross-sell.
5. **State-by-state awareness.** You can only treat patients where you have a licensed provider; campaigns and eligibility have to be geo-gated to your covered states.

## How to run the evaluation

Put your shortlist through the same test:

- Ask them to **sequence a LegitScript certification** — the answer reveals real telehealth experience.
- Ask to see a **condition-specific funnel** they've built and its conversion rate.
- Ask for **paying-patient CAC** (not consult CAC) on a real account.
- Ask how they'd handle **the async-to-sync handoff** — identity verification, provider review, pharmacy fulfillment — where most telehealth funnels leak.

## The bottom line

The best telehealth marketing agency for your brand is the one that's fluent in certification, builds condition-specific funnels, measures true paying-patient CAC, and has a retention plan. For most DTC telehealth brands, that's a health-specialist performance partner.
