If you've ever launched a telehealth ad account and watched it get suspended on day one, this is almost always why: you weren't LegitScript-certified. It's the single most common reason telehealth and online-pharmacy brands can't spend a dollar on the major platforms - and it's completely avoidable if you plan for it.

Here's what LegitScript certification is, who needs it, what it costs, and how to sequence it so it doesn't cost you a launch window.

*Certification requirements and eligibility change - confirm current details with LegitScript and each platform before you build campaigns. This is practitioner guidance, not legal advice.*

## What is LegitScript certification?

LegitScript certification is a **third-party accreditation that verifies an online pharmacy or telehealth provider operates legally.** It's the trust credential the ad platforms lean on so they don't have to individually vet every healthcare advertiser. Google, Meta, Bing, and TikTok all require it before you can promote prescription-related services.

Think of it as the gatekeeper for the entire regulated-health advertising ecosystem: no certification, no ads.

## Who actually needs LegitScript certification?

You need it if you advertise, in most markets:

- **Telehealth / online prescribing** (including GLP-1, TRT, mental health, dermatology)
- **Online pharmacies** and drug sellers
- **Addiction treatment** (a separate certification track)
- **Many supplement and health services** on Meta, Google, and Microsoft

You generally **don't** need it for pure education, brand awareness, or services that never touch prescribing - which is the main lever for brands that want to run Meta ads while their certification is in process. See how that plays out for [telehealth on Meta](/advertising/telehealth-on-meta/) and [telehealth on Google](/advertising/telehealth-on-google/).

## What does LegitScript certification cost, and how long does it take?

Budget realistically:

- **~$975 application fee**, plus roughly **$2,150 annually per website** (addiction/MAT programs run higher, into multiple thousands) - see [LegitScript's published fee schedule](https://help.legitscript.com/s/article/How-much-does-certification-cost) for the current numbers by program.
- **4–8 weeks lead time** from application to approval - sometimes longer.
- Ongoing **monitoring and annual re-certification.**

The process itself: application → licensing and ownership documentation → due-diligence review → approval → ongoing monitoring. The paperwork stage is where most brands stall, so get your licensing and corporate docs in order before you apply.

## LegitScript vs. Google's own certification: don't confuse the two

This trips up a lot of founders. On **Google**, LegitScript is only step one. It's a two-step process:

1. **Third-party accreditation** - LegitScript (or NABP in some cases).
2. **Google's own Prescription drug services certification**, applied for at the child-account level.

Pharmaceutical manufacturers use a *separate* Google certification track entirely. On **Meta**, you need active LegitScript certification **and** Meta authorization, targeting only the countries where you're certified (US/CA/NZ), gated to 18+.

## How to sequence certification so it doesn't cost you a launch

The mistake we see most: brands treat certification as something to figure out *after* they build campaigns, then lose 4–8 weeks of runway waiting. Do it the other way around:

1. **Start LegitScript before you build anything else** - it's the long pole in the tent.
2. **While it's in process, run compliant education and brand-awareness ads** that don't name a drug or promote prescribing, so you're building audiences and pixels the whole time.
3. **Get your provider network and state licensing squared away in parallel** - you can only treat patients where you have a licensed provider.
4. **Then complete platform-specific certification** (Google's Prescription drug services, Meta authorization) and flip on the Rx campaigns.

Done this way, certification runs in the background instead of blocking your launch.

## The bottom line

LegitScript certification is table stakes for advertising telehealth, and the map keeps changing - 2026 saw eligibility extended to new markets, and platform rules shift constantly. Budget ~$975 + ~$2,150/year per site and 4–8 weeks, start it before anything else, and run compliant education ads in the meantime so you're never sitting idle.
